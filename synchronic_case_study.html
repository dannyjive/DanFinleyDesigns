Synchronic Services Website Redesign

Designed and developed a responsive business website for Synchronic Services to improve credibility, communicate services clearly, and generate customer inquiries.

Role: Designer & Front-End Developer

Duration: 4 Weeks

Tools: Figma, Photoshop, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript

The Challenge

Synchronic Services needed a modern website that accurately reflected their business and helped potential clients quickly understand the services they offered.

  • Outdated visual design
  • Unclear service descriptions
  • Limited mobile optimization
  • Difficult contact experience

Discovery & Research

To understand both business and user needs, I conducted client interviews and distributed questionnaires.

Questions Asked

  • Who is your target audience?
  • What services are most important to highlight?
  • What action should visitors take?
  • What websites inspire you?
  • What problems exist with your current website?

Research Findings

Users Needed Clarity

Potential customers needed a faster understanding of available services and business value.

Mobile Experience Needed Improvement

The website needed to perform well across phones, tablets, and desktops.

Trust Needed To Be Established Quickly

Visual design needed to communicate professionalism and reliability.

Information Architecture

Based on research findings, I created a simplified sitemap focused on helping users find information quickly. 

Home
├── Services
├── About
├── Testimonials
└── Contact

Wireframing

Low-fidelity wireframes helped establish content hierarchy, navigation patterns, and call-to-action placement before visual design began.

Goals

  • Highlight services immediately
  • Create clear navigation
  • Reduce cognitive load
  • Increase inquiry conversions

Visual Design Exploration

Multiple design directions were created and reviewed with the client.

Option A

Corporate and minimal.

Option B

Friendly and approachable.

Final Direction

The selected direction balanced professionalism, trust, and accessibility.

Development

After design approval, the website was developed using modern front-end technologies.

Technology Stack

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • Bootstrap
  • JavaScript

Key Features

  • Responsive design
  • Mobile navigation
  • Performance optimization
  • Contact form integration
  • Cross-browser compatibility

Testing & Iteration

The site was tested across multiple devices and browsers to ensure a consistent experience.

  • Desktop testing
  • Tablet testing
  • Mobile testing
  • Form validation testing
  • Performance checks

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge

During deployment, DNS changes temporarily affected website availability.

Solution

Worked with hosting support, verified DNS records, and restored proper routing to minimize downtime.

Results

  • Modernized company image
  • Improved mobile experience
  • Simplified customer journey
  • Clearer communication of services
  • Improved overall usability
"The new website better represents our business and provides a much stronger first impression."

Alignment with Amazon Leadership Principles

Customer Obsession

Started with stakeholder interviews and focused on user needs throughout the project.

Ownership

Managed research, design, development, deployment, and support.

Invent & Simplify

Reduced complexity through simplified navigation and content organization.

Deliver Results

Successfully launched a responsive website that met both business and user goals.
